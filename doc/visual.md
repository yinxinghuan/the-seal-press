# Visual Bible — The Seal Press

## 1. Visual thesis
- A future-archaeology press turns one nightly thought into a tactile museum relic; the memorable moment is a newly fired seal emerging from blackness.
## 2. Composition and camera
- Portrait-first at 390×844 and 320×568; square top-down artifact photography remains the focal surface, with quiet field/altar navigation around it.
## 3. Color
- Near-black #0A0508, cream #F4E8D0, dusty pink #F5B1C7 and terracotta #B85A3A; generated seals use material color against pure black.
## 4. Typography
- Cormorant Garamond supplies archaeological/editorial voice; Montserrat handles compact controls and metadata with system CJK fallback.
## 5. Shape, material, and lighting
- Carved discs, hexagons, arches and tablets; recessed grooves, aged clay/porcelain/obsidian and flat raking sidelight.
## 6. Characters, environments, and assets
- No characters; generation is text mode at 512×512, one centered artifact, pure-black full bleed, direct overhead view and no ground plane.
## 7. UI and icons
- Custom geometric SVG icons, thin cream rules and restrained labels; touch targets remain at least 44×44 and functional Emoji are forbidden.
## 8. Motion and VFX
- Press, kiln glow and reveal use weighty 120–900 ms motion; reduced-motion keeps state changes without pulsing or scale travel.
## 9. References translated into principles
- Museum object photography and excavated seals provide material authority; no identifiable historical artifact is copied.
## 10. Anti-patterns
- No glossy game cards, raised/painted carvings, grey studio sweep, floating props, extra pseudo-text or neon fantasy ornament.
## 11. Vertical-slice acceptance
- Entry, press, reveal, field and altar remain legible on narrow mobile; real task `mt_442ec440ffb1a8c3a0a1be42ca96cf00` passed with one incised artifact, pure-black edges and only the requested marks.
